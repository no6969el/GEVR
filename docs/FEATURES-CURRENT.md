## vr456.3

- **Moonraker** - small circular scope lens only. No big front grill screen. Two Moonrakers, two lenses. Look through the ring.
- **Planted view** - no walk bob, no landing dip, no gun-hand sway.
- **Snap-turn** - pause VR Settings. Pick an angle.
- **Stick click** - weapon wheel (circle default). Pause VR Settings **STICK WHEEL** can set **Weapon Vert**.
- **Holster** - same-gun hip grip swaps. All Guns cycle can give a second copy while the hip keeps its own. Switch prefers inventory over the hip gun.
- **Weapons** - Left X is the next weapon on the left hand. Right A is the next weapon on the right hand.
- **Watch** - cuff picker Laser / Magnet / Repel. Unlimited. Does not crash. Y opens the watch. Cuff grab detonates remotes, otherwise the laser comes from the cuff. No detonator in the weapon cycle.
- **Tank** - auto-reloads the next round after you fire.
- **Aimer** - red and green sit on the first hit. Hole under the dot. Left-handed sniper works.
- **Left throw** - bomb case and plastique sit in the left like the right.
- **HD memos** - memo cameras look sharp.
- **Rockets** - stay locked to the launcher, not your head.
- **Busy rooms / quit** - heavy rooms stay at full speed. Quitting actually quits.
- **Visual mode** - VR, XR (smaller screen, black outline), or Flat on the monitor. Change the row in **GEVR Settings** and **Apply**. Apply saves and relaunches into that mode.
- **Frame rate** - follows the headset by default. Fixed 90 is still available.
- **Supersample** starts at 3. **Filter** starts on bilinear. Point is still available.
- **Monitor** - Both, Left, Right, or Off. Off blanks the mirror while you stay in the headset.
- **GEVR Settings** - on Mission Select, next to Select Mission, Multiplayer, and Cheat Options. It is not named Options.
- **Reload** - You do not have to reload by hand. B reloads any gun the normal way. Hold any gun to your upper chest and press the grab button to reload it. On a gun with a visible magazine, grab that magazine with the grip button. Pistols, the shotgun, the sniper, and other guns with no visible magazine reload across the chest or with B. A handle grab swaps hands and does not reload. Handheld guns do not reload on their own.
- **Left hand** - a left-hand gun fires on its own.
- **Ammo** - digits sit on the grip and read from behind the gun. Flat mode keeps the corner count. Weapon pictures sit on the lifting hand, including the grenade and mines.
- **Mines** - remote, proximity, and timed mines draw in the hand like the grenade.
- **Swing** - uses the held weapon's damage. Shooting does not make the knife swing by itself.
- **Spread** - stays around the controller aim.
- **Janus meeting** - the crowd stops respawning after the meeting.
- **HD textures** - PNG pack in `hdtextures\GOLDENEYE` next to `goldeneye.exe`. `GOLDENEYE_HIRESTEXTURES.hts` is the wrong file. GEVR does not ship the pack.
