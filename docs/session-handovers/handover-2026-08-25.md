## The owner's question, answered

> *"Perfect dark should work the same as goldeneye so I am unsure why this isn't
> just copy paste."*

**They edit `bgunSwivel` from inside the game, where `camGetWorldToScreenMtxf()`
is in scope, and PROJECT a world ray through the game's own camera matrix — zero
tuning constants. We compute our aim in `rt64_render_context.cpp`, where that
matrix is not in scope, so we ESTIMATED the projection with one scalar
(`GE_VR_AIM_GAIN`) and a pile of sign knobs.** Everything else follows from that.

> *"There is something that is locking the movement."*

**Three things, and none of them needs a rewrite of the movement code:**

1. **The controllers never bind.** `handValid=0` on all 46 samples, `bound
   profile = NONE BOUND` with four profiles suggested ok. **A hand that never
   arrives cannot move anything.** (`6D-14` → `P0-1`)
2. **The aim write is `REFUSED` every frame**, so both integrators decay to
   screen centre and every shot leaves along the view axis. **That is the
   "shoots where I look" report exactly.** (`158` → `P0-2`, `P1-2`)
3. **The `[3, w-4]` clamp** — real, already unclamped behind `-FreeAim`, and
   **the smallest of the three.** It only bites once 1 and 2 are fixed.

## The five borrowables, ranked (`160` §2)

- **B1 — project the ray through the game's own camera matrix.** PD:
  `muzzlepos + dir*100000` → `camGetWorldToScreenMtxf()` → pixels → NDC.
  **Zero free parameters, exact at any FOV.** Deletes `-AimGain`, `-AimSignX/Y`,
  `-AimFwdAxis`, `-AimFwdNeg`, `-AimBasis`. Our candidate is
  **`transform3Dto2DCoords`** — a read, not a launch.
- **B2 — drive BOTH integrators.** PD pre-loads `crosspossum` **and**
  `crosssum2` two lines apart. **We write one.** So the gun's pointing and the
  bullet disagree by construction. Four lines.
- **B3 — the muzzle comes out of the gun model** (`MODELPART_GUN_MUZZLEPOS`),
  not out of a knob. **And it exposed a live bug: `muzzle_origin` still uses the
  barrel axis `158` §3 FALSIFIED, while `aim_screen_ndc` uses the derived one.**
  Hidden only because `-MuzzleExt` defaults to 0.
- **B4 — aim at the laser dot's real hit point** when there is one. The exact
  cure for muzzle parallax up close. **`102` called A2 "a nicety"; it is prior
  art's aiming INPUT.**
- **B5 — the clamp split**: clamp the DRAWN crosshair, leave the SHOT free.
  `153` §8's plan, still unbuilt.

## Also found

- **One gain for two axes** — GE scales X by `screen_width` and Y by
  `screen_height`, so a single `GE_VR_AIM_GAIN` cannot be right on both.
- **No NDC clamp** — `fwd < 0.05` refuses, but a divisor just above it still
  throws `ndc=(-8.5,-17.9)`. That is the recorded "shooting the ground".
- **`GE_VR_AIM_FWD_NEG` defaults to 1** and `-AimFwdNeg 0` is what cleared the
  refusal. **A trap as shipped.**
- **PD's recoil is 100% host-side** (their own comment), so it is close to a
  drop-in for us. Recorded at `P3-1`, not a priority.
