# How to play

GEVR is GoldenEye in real VR, on PC. You bring a USA ROM you already own. It is Beta.

**Latest:** [vr456.1](https://github.com/no6969el/GEVR/releases/tag/vr456.1). Zip: **`GEVR-Beta-vr456.1-win64.zip`**. The zip has no ROM and no HD texture pack. Install: [README](../README.md#install).

Headset: run `Start-GEVR.bat`. No headset: run `Play-on-monitor.bat`. Point the starter at your USA `.z64`. Recenter with both thumbstick clicks. Do not upload ROM files.

Ammo digits, the hip holster, weapon pictures, and chest reload stay on without boot knobs. Shooting over a ledge no longer deflects down.

**Left X** is the previous weapon on the left hand. **Right A** is the next weapon on the right hand. **B** reloads any gun. Upper chest plus grab reloads. A visible magazine reloads with a grip. Pistol, shotgun, sniper, and other guns with no visible magazine reload across the chest or with **B**.

Button tables: [Controls](CONTROLS.md). Picture options: [GEVR Settings](GEVR-SETTINGS.md). HD textures are beta. They can stutter, including on a fast card. PNG packs go in `hdtextures\GOLDENEYE` next to `goldeneye.exe`. `GOLDENEYE_HIRESTEXTURES.hts` is the wrong file. Steps: [HD textures](GEVR-SETTINGS.md#hd-textures).

More: [Beta notes](BETA.md), [features](../FEATURES.md), [how to report](../CONTRIBUTING.md). License map: [LICENSE-MAP.md](../LICENSE-MAP.md).

Lab notes: numbered `docs/NNN-*.md` files are historical research. Not the player guide.
