# Start here

You found GEVR. GoldenEye in real VR, on PC, with a USA ROM you already own. It is Beta. It is worth playing.

**Play:** [GEVR Beta vr454](https://github.com/no6969el/GEVR/releases/latest) (GitHub Latest). Zip: **`GEVR-Beta-vr454-win64.zip`**. The zip has no ROM and no HD texture pack. Tag: [vr454](https://github.com/no6969el/GEVR/releases/tag/vr454). Actions, the vr454 how-to, and install: [README](../README.md#vr-actions).

**Players and testers:** start at the [README](../README.md). Then [controls](CONTROLS.md), [GEVR Settings](GEVR-SETTINGS.md), [Beta notes](BETA.md), and [features](../FEATURES.md). How to report: [CONTRIBUTING](../CONTRIBUTING.md). License map: [LICENSE-MAP.md](../LICENSE-MAP.md).

Headset: `Start-GEVR.bat`. No headset: `Play-on-monitor.bat`, or choose **Flat** in GEVR Settings and Apply. Do not upload ROM files.

PNG texture packs go in `hdtextures\GOLDENEYE` next to `goldeneye.exe`. `GOLDENEYE_HIRESTEXTURES.hts` is the wrong file.

Stay tuned. Star the repo and [follow @no6969el](https://github.com/no6969el).

Lab notes: numbered `docs/NNN-*.md` files are historical research. Not the player guide.
