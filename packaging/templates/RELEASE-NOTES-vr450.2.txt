GEVR Beta vr450.2
=================

Wear-focused update. Bring your own USA ROM. No ROM in the zip.

Live: https://github.com/no6969el/GEVR/releases/tag/vr450.2
Zip:  https://github.com/no6969el/GEVR/releases/download/vr450.2/GEVR-Beta-vr450.2-win64.zip

1) In-app Update
----------------
Open GevrRomStarter - it checks GitHub Latest.
Click Update once to pull this zip, keep saves / prefs / your .z64,
then launch Start-GEVR.bat.

The starter finds goldeneye.exe in the same folder and sets the game
path for you. Do not double-click goldeneye.exe.

Each Beta tag bumps a ship stamp so the first launch after an update
rebuilds the image cache once from your same ROM. Saves stay.

2) What is new in vr450.2 (outcomes)
------------------------------------
Keepers are hard-coded in the binary. They stay on with a clean launch.

- Cuff / watch — left-hand cuff stays with the controller through stages
- FREEARM (#109) — two-handed NPC / weapon poses look better
- VTXFIXREFS (#117) — more stable Surface geometry
- Door snap — door-edge aim / hit more dependable around room edges
- False doors — quieter decoy / false door presentation
- Corpse pass — dead bodies jam doors less often
- Re-grab — thrown remote / prox mines can be picked back up
- Mines — stick to guards, follow them, stay visible while carried
- Landmark / aim scale / embed eye — aim and world marks stay readable
