GEVR Beta vr445.1
==================

Small update on vr445. Bring your own USA ROM.

1) In-app Update
----------------
Open GevrRomStarter - it checks GitHub Latest.
Click Update once to pull this zip, keep saves / prefs / your .z64,
then launch Start-GEVR.bat.

Each Beta tag bumps a ship stamp so the first launch after an update
rebuilds the image cache once from your same ROM. Saves stay.

2) Footnote on vr445.1
----------------------
Gunfire fixed (GitHub #84): On PC, rifle guards were using pistol lean /
single-shot cadence because equipped weapon props were misread on 64-bit.
They now pick the correct rifle fire tables and cadence (auto/burst where
the weapon expects it).

Known / in progress (GitHub #82 Statue Janus): The meet/scene not starting
at Janus is still open; we are working on it alongside this cut. This tag
does not fix Janus.

Everything else is still vr445 (ADS walk/crouch, dual-wield default,
Hertz follow, monitor in VR, SKYINF, mine/black flicker quieted for
now by the temporary no-modem hide -- GitHub #55).

3) Play
-------
Start-GEVR.bat (uses GevrRomStarter). USA .z64.
Do not double-click goldeneye.exe.
