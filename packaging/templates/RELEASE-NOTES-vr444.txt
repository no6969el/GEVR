GEVR Beta vr444
===============

From-source GoldenEye 007 in real OpenXR VR. Bring your own USA ROM.

Major glitch cut after vr443.1. Cooked on SimRig 2026-09-20/21.

1) In-app Update
----------------
Open GevrRomStarter - it checks GitHub Latest.
Click Update once to pull this zip, keep saves / prefs / your .z64,
then launch Start-GEVR.bat.

Each Beta tag bumps a ship stamp so the first launch after an update
rebuilds the image cache once from your same ROM. Saves stay.

2) What is new in vr444 (on the box)
------------------------------------
- Quieter covert-modem scrap. The stuck floor scrap / flicker is
  quieted. The modem in your hand and the screens stay on.
  (Dam blue / dish-on-glass depth still cooking.)
- Throwables live in your hand. Grenades, mines, plastique, and the
  covert modem appear in the grip and leave from there. Grenades feel
  small; mines a touch larger.
- Hand cue cubes hide while that hand holds a weapon. Empty / fists
  show the cube again. The cube is smaller than before.
- Tap A for the next gun. Tap left-controller X for the previous gun.
  (A full weapon-wheel HUD is still cooking.)
- Follows your headset refresh rate (72 / 80 / 90 / 120 as your runtime
  reports) instead of pinning 90. High Hz is still Beta - report odd
  feel if you see it.

Still in from vr443.1: physical walk moves you (not the world), guns
stick to your hands, look-right VR Settings, Dam sky pin, dual-wield,
BYO-ROM cache, in-app Update.

3) Play
-------
Start-GEVR.bat (uses GevrRomStarter). USA .z64.
Do not double-click goldeneye.exe.

https://github.com/no6969el/GEVR/issues/new/choose
Do not upload your ROM.
